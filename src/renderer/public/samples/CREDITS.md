# Built-in drum samples

These sounds ship with StemDeck. Both sets are free to redistribute.

## TR-808 (`tr808/`)

Recordings of a real Roland TR-808 (serial no. 103852) made in 1994 by
Michael Fischer of Technopolis, who released them free for everyone. This
selection comes from the TidalCycles packaging,
https://github.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer, published under CC0 1.0
(public domain) — see `tr808/LICENSE`. Fischer's original notes are in
`tr808/README-original.txt`. File names keep his scheme: `bd5075` = bass
drum at TONE 50 %, DECAY 75 % (`10` means 100 %); snares are TONE + SNAPPY.

## uzu (`uzu/`)

The uzu drum kit, https://github.com/tidalcycles/uzu-drumkit, released into
the public domain under the Unlicense — see `uzu/LICENSE`. Sounds by Mot4i,
Switch Angel and Ludens. Its processed Amen-break loop is deliberately not
included: that is a copyrighted 1969 recording.
